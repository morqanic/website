Morgan

Hello!! I am Morgan, one of the Education Directors (ex Conference Director, ex CTF Subcom).

My goal for next year is to increase outreach, strengthen our community and foster our hacker culture!

I tutor COMP1337, COMP6[48]41 and COMP1511, if you want to see me teach and inspire check out my BSides Canberra Talk!

Outreach

I love inspiring and helping others! A big part of this is how we make others welcomed and included.
High school outreach and CTFs is a great way to not only introduce security to everyone, but also to train ourselves. While not everyone can prepare a workshop at a uni level, preparing security workshops and CTFs for high school students feels alot more approachable!

Community

It is really important that everyone feels like they belong!
This means being proactive and purposefull while talking and introducing yourself to new people! Additionally a big initative is supporting G11 (Women CTF team).
Additionally I love connecting UNSW to the other security societies by organsing collab events (like the physical security workshop) as well as at conferences. I've organised 5 roadtrips for SecSoc in the past 4 years as I think it is so imporant to know and be confident with each other.

Hacking

Security is fun, but hacking is even cooler! I want people to question the assumptions arround them and work on really cool projects! This will involved creating more time for people to work on projects and ensuring our events have a low barrier of entry (eg highschool level CTF's that can be done for outreach but also as a introductory CTF).
